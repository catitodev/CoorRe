Provable decisions for AI agents.

CoorRe is a verifiability engine: it turns the evidence and decisions of a process into signed, anchored proof that anyone can check. On Solana, the program enforces who can act and the limit of autonomy; above the limit, only the human approver can release the payment.

      Verify

      Check an audit trail without trusting whoever produced it. The evidence, signatures and hashes are checked in this browser with the same Rust code as the command line; only public account addresses are sent to a Solana RPC to read what the chain recorded.

      Public

      available

      Anyone checks a trail on this page or on the command line, with no login and no account. Whoever holds an audit bundle and its documents can verify it; the chain holds only hashes, keys, states and amounts.

      Permissioned

      roadmap

      Views by role and viewing keys, so each party sees only what its role needs. Not part of this build.

            What verification proves

            • Who signed each step, with which key, and that the key held that role on-chain.
            • That nothing in the trail changed after it was anchored.
            • That every automated decision follows from the rule named in the evidence.
            • That no approval above the mandate happened without a person.

            What it does not prove

            • That a document is genuine: the demo documents are synthetic; a real deployment needs documents signed by their issuer or registry.
            • That a rule is the right policy for an organisation or a law.
            • Who holds a key: the chain proves which keys acted, not which people.
            • Anything on mainnet, which is a next step: this build runs on devnet only, with demo keys operated by the CoorRe team.
